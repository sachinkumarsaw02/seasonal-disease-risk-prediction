PROJECT OVERVIEW

Predictive Analysis of Seasonal Diseases in Rural/Urban Area

This web-based academic prototype uses environmental and location-based inputs to estimate a seasonal disease-risk category.

Objectives

  • Predict seasonal environmental disease risk using machine learning.
  • Identify potentially higher-risk locations in the prototype dataset.
  • Display the selected location on an interactive map.
  • Provide general health-awareness information.

Technology Stack

PythonFlaskScikit-learn PandasNumPyHTML CSSJavaScriptLeaflet.js

Important limitation

The included dataset is synthetic and intended for academic demonstration. The model should not be used for real-world medical diagnosis, public-health decisions, or individual health-risk assessment.
